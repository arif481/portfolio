Research

NIT Silchar · Summer 2026 · Submitted to E2A 2027 (Springer LNEE)

Reliable code generation on low-cost hardware

Small code models get many tasks wrong on the first try. I tested a cheap fix on Qwen2.5-Coder 1.5B, 3B and 7B: sample up to 20 programs and keep the first one that passes the examples already given in the task.

At equal cost it gained about ten times as much as SymLoop, a neurosymbolic repair loop I built with CrossHair and Hypothesis. It never replaced a correct first answer with a wrong one, and every experiment ran on Kaggle's free GPUs.

  • +4.3 to +9.8HumanEval+ pass@1
  • +8.5 to +10.2MBPP+ held-out pass@1
  • p < 0.02all six EvalPlus gains
  • 0correct answers lost
